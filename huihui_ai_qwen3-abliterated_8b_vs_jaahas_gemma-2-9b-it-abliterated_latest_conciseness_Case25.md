These structures exhibit precise orientation to solstices, equinoxes, and stellar constellations, suggesting a universal preoccupation with cosmic cycles. The Mayan Long Count calendar’s 5125-year cycle, the Inca’s geoglyphic alignment with the Pleiades, and the Giza pyramids’ 2π ratio to Earth’s circumference all imply a collective memory of a cataclysmic event—likely a comet impact or axial tilt shift—whose effects were encoded in their design. These features collectively indicate a lost epoch of human civilization predating written records, where astronomical knowledge was preserved through monumental architecture as a symbolic record of a shared cataclysm.
