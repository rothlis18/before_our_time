### Architectural, Symbolic, and Astronomical Parallels Among Ancient Structures

#### 1. **Architectural Parallels**

- **Giza Pyramids (Egypt):**
  - **Great Pyramid of Giza:** Aligned with the pole star (Thuban) at the time of its construction, indicating a sophisticated understanding of celestial mechanics.
  - **Pyramid of Khafre:** Also aligned with Thuban, showcasing consistent astronomical knowledge across multiple structures.
  - **Pyramid of Menkaure:** Smaller in scale but still aligned with the same celestial axis, suggesting a standardized architectural template.

- **Teotihuacan (Mexico):**
  - **Pyramid of the Sun:** Base dimensions of 230 meters by 230 meters, with a height of 66 meters, creating a 1:1.5 ratio. This ratio is similar to the Great Pyramid of Giza.
  - **Pyramid of the Moon:** Base dimensions of 187 meters by 187 meters, with a height of 42 meters, maintaining a similar 1:1.5 ratio.

- **Inca Temples:**
  - **Machu Picchu:** Utilizes precise stonework and alignment with celestial bodies, particularly the sun and solstices. The Intihuatana stone marks the summer solstice.
  - **Ollantaytambo:** Features stepped terraces and temples aligned with celestial events, indicating a deep understanding of solar and lunar cycles.

- **Mayan Step Pyramids:**
  - **Chichen Itza:** El Castillo pyramid has a 45-degree slope and a serpent motif that aligns with the equinoxes, creating a shadow effect that resembles a descending serpent.
  - **Tulum:** Features stepped structures and temples aligned with celestial bodies, particularly the sun and moon.

- **Angkor Wat (Cambodia):**
  - **Main Temple:** Oriented towards the east, with a central tower that aligns with the rising sun. The temple complex is vast, covering 162.6 hectares.
  - **Banteay Srei:** Features intricate carvings and alignments with celestial bodies, particularly the moon and planets.
