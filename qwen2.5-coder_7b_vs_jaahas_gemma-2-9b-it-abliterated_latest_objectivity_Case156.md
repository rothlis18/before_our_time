### Architectural and Symbolic Parallels

1. **Pyramids and Step Pyramids**:
   - **Structure and Orientation**: The pyramids and step pyramids are designed with precise geometric proportions and orientations. The Giza pyramids, for instance, are aligned with the cardinal directions and the stars, particularly the pole star. This suggests a sophisticated understanding of astronomy and a belief in celestial bodies as symbols of divine authority and cosmic order.
   - **Symbolism**: The pyramids often represent the transition from life to the afterlife, with their stepped design symbolizing the journey through the underworld. The use of stone, a durable material, underscores the belief in an eternal, unchanging realm.

2. **Inca and Mayan Temples**:
   - **Alignment with Celestial Bodies**: Both Inca and Mayan temples are meticulously aligned with celestial bodies, particularly the sun and the stars. This alignment was not merely decorative but served practical and religious purposes. For example, the Inca Temple of the Sun in Cusco was aligned with the summer solstice, indicating a deep understanding of solar cycles and their influence on agriculture and calendar systems.
   - **Symbolism**: The temples were not just places of worship but also centers of power and governance. Their grandeur and complexity suggest a society that believed in the power of divine intervention and the importance of maintaining cosmic balance.
