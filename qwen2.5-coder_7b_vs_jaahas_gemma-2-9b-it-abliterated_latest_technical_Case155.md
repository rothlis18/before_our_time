### Architectural Parallels

1. **Massive Stone Blocks:**
   - **Giza:** The Great Pyramid of Giza is composed of over 2.3 million stone blocks, each averaging 2.5 to 15 tons. The limestone used has a density of about 2.75 g/cm³ and a compressive strength of around 200 MPa.
   - **Teotihuacan:** The Pyramid of the Sun at Teotihuacan is made of volcanic tuff, with a density of about 2.3 g/cm³ and a compressive strength of around 100 MPa.
   - **Mayan Step Pyramids:** The Chichen Itza pyramid is built from limestone, with a density of 2.75 g/cm³ and a compressive strength of 200 MPa.
   - **Inca Temples:** The Inca used granite, with a density of 2.75 g/cm³ and a compressive strength of 250 MPa.
   - **Angkor Wat:** The temple is built from sandstone, with a density of 2.6 g/cm³ and a compressive strength of 150 MPa.

2. **Logistical Challenges:**
   - **Transportation Distance:** The transportation of massive stone blocks over long distances required significant resources. For example, the limestone used at Giza was quarried near Tura, about 80 km away.
   - **Labor Availability:** The construction of these structures required large labor forces. The Giza pyramids were built by a workforce of around 100,000 people over a period of 20 years.

### Symbolic and Astronomical Parallels

1. **Astronomical Alignment:**
   - **Giza:** The pyramids are aligned with the stars, with the Sphinx facing the constellation of Leo.
   - **Teotihuacan:** The Pyramid of the Sun is aligned with the summer solstice sunrise.
   - **Mayan Step Pyramids:** The Chichen Itza pyramid has a spiral staircase that aligns with the movement of the sun.
   - **Inca Temples:** The Inca used solar and lunar symbolism in their architecture.
   - **Angkor Wat:** The temple is aligned with the rising and setting of the sun and moon.

### Resource Trade-offs

1. **Material Choices:**
   - **Giza:** Limestone was chosen for its availability and ease of transportation.
   - **Teotihuacan:** Volcanic tuff was used due to its abundance and ease of quarrying.
   - **Mayan Step Pyramids:** Limestone was used for its durability and availability.
   - **Inca Temples:** Granite was chosen for its strength and durability.
   - **Angkor Wat:** Sandstone was used for its availability and ease of transportation.

2. **Trade-offs:**
   - **Monumental Scale vs. Astronomical Alignment:** The Giza pyramids prioritize monumental scale, while Teotihuacan emphasizes astronomical alignment.
   - **Resource Availability:** The choice of materials was influenced by the availability of resources in each region.
