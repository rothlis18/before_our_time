The alignment of the three major pyramids (Giza, Teotihuacan, and the Pyramid of the Sun) with cardinal directions, coupled with the presence of similar astronomical alignments in Inca temples, Mayan step pyramids, and Angkor Wat, suggests a shared astronomical knowledge base. This knowledge base, likely developed during a pre-recorded epoch, could have been transmitted through cultural diffusion or a more ancient, interconnected civilization. The consistent use of pyramid structures across geographically disparate locations, each serving as a celestial observatory and monument, points to a collective memory of a cataclysmic event, possibly a celestial alignment or impact, which profoundly shaped human understanding of the cosmos and its cyclical nature.
