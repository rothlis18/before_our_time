**Analysis:**

1. **Architectural Parallels:**
   - **Giza, Teotihuacan, Pyramid of the Sun:** All three are large-scale stone structures with precise alignment to cardinal directions (within 3° of accuracy). They exhibit similar construction techniques, using massive stone blocks.
   - **Inca temples, Mayan step pyramids, Angkor Wat:** These structures also demonstrate precise alignment, often with astronomical bodies. They employ stepped construction, unlike the smooth-sided pyramids, but share the use of large stone blocks.

2. **Symbolic Parallels:**
   - **Pyramids:** All pyramids share a common symbolism of ascension, with their peaks often aligned with stars or celestial bodies.
   - **Inca temples & Mayan step pyramids:** Both often incorporate astronomical symbols and alignments, with staircases representing cosmic paths or mountains connecting earth to sky.
   - **Angkor Wat:** While not a pyramid, it shares the symbolism of ascension through its central tower and astronomical alignments, particularly with the sun and moon.

3. **Astronomical Parallels:**
   - **Pyramids:** The Great Pyramid of Giza is aligned with the constellation Orion, while the Pyramid of the Sun at Teotihuacan is aligned with the Pleiades. Both are believed to mark significant astronomical events.
   - **Inca temples & Mayan step pyramids:** Many Inca and Mayan structures align with solstices, equinoxes, or significant stars/constellations, such as the alignment of Chichen Itza with the equinox sunrise.
   - **Angkor Wat:** Aligned with the sun's path at the equinoxes, it also incorporates astronomical symbols like the nagas (serpent deities) representing the ecliptic.

**Implications:**

- The precision of alignment and construction techniques suggest a shared understanding of astronomy, mathematics, and engineering.
- The consistent use of large stone blocks implies access to significant resources and labor organization.
- The symbolic parallels suggest shared cosmological beliefs and rituals.
- These shared features imply an unrecorded epoch of human civilization predating known history, with cultural exchange and knowledge sharing across continents.

**Constraints:**

- The lack of written records makes this epoch difficult to prove definitively.
- Cultural exchange and independent invention cannot be definitively distinguished.
- The exact timeline and nature of this shared epoch remain speculative.
