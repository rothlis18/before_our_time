### Architectural, Symbolic, and Astronomical Parallels Among Ancient Pyramids and Temples

#### Architectural Parallels

1. **Alignment and Orientation**:
   - **Giza Pyramids**: The Great Pyramid of Giza is aligned with the cardinal directions, with its entrance facing north. The alignment of the pyramids with the stars, particularly Orion's Belt, suggests a sophisticated understanding of celestial mechanics.
   - **Teotihuacan**: The Pyramid of the Sun and the Pyramid of the Moon are aligned with the cardinal directions and the Pleiades, indicating a similar celestial orientation.
   - **Inca Temples**: Machu Picchu and other Inca sites exhibit precise alignment with the solstices and equinoxes, reflecting a deep knowledge of solar cycles.
   - **Mayan Step Pyramids**: Tikal and Chichen Itza feature stepped pyramids aligned with celestial bodies, particularly the sun and Venus.
   - **Angkor Wat**: The temple complex is oriented towards the east, facing the rising sun, and is aligned with the stars, including the constellation Draco.

2. **Structural Design**:
   - **Giza Pyramids**: The pyramids are massive structures with precise angles and slopes, suggesting advanced engineering techniques.
   - **Teotihuacan**: The Avenue of the Dead and the alignment of the pyramids indicate a planned urban layout with astronomical significance.
   - **Inca Temples**: The use of large stone blocks and precise cuts, as seen in Machu Picchu, demonstrate advanced masonry skills.
   - **Mayan Step Pyramids**: The stepped design and alignment with celestial bodies reflect a complex cosmology.
   - **Angkor Wat**: The temple's layout and orientation towards the east and celestial bodies indicate a sophisticated understanding of architecture and astronomy.
