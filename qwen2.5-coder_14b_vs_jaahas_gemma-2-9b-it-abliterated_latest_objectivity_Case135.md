### Architectural, Symbolic, and Astronomical Parallels Among Ancient Structures

#### 1. **Giza Pyramids (Egypt)**

- **Symbolic Features:** The pyramid's shape represents the sun god Ra, with the capstone symbolizing the sky.
- **Astronomical Features:** The pyramid's shadow at noon on the summer solstice aligns with the Sphinx's face, suggesting a celestial alignment.

#### 2. **Teotihuacan and the Pyramid of the Sun (Mexico)**
- **Architectural Features:** The Pyramid of the Sun is aligned with the cardinal directions and the Pleiades. The Avenue of the Dead runs east-west.
- **Symbolic Features:** The pyramid is associated with the sun god Tonatiuh, and the city's layout reflects a cosmological order.
- **Astronomical Features:** The pyramid's alignment with the Pleiades and the sun's zenith at the summer solstice.

#### 3. **Inca Temples (Peru)**
- **Architectural Features:** Machu Picchu's Intihuatana stone aligns with the sun's position at the winter solstice and summer solstice.
- **Symbolic Features:** The site is dedicated to the sun god Inti, and the terraces reflect agricultural practices.
- **Astronomical Features:** The Intihuatana stone marks the sun's position at the solstices and equinoxes.

#### 4. **Mayan Step Pyramids (Mexico)**
- **Architectural Features:** Chichen Itza's El Castillo is aligned with the Pleiades and the Ecliptic. The pyramid's shadow creates the appearance of a serpent descending during the equinox.
- **Symbolic Features:** The pyramid is associated with the feathered serpent god Kukulkan and the sun god.
- **Astronomical Features:** The equinox alignment and the shadow play.

#### 5. **Angkor Wat (Cambodia)**
- **Architectural Features:** The temple is aligned with the cardinal directions and the Milky Way. The layout reflects a complex cosmological order.
- **Symbolic Features:** The temple is dedicated to the Hindu god Vishnu and represents Mount Meru.
- **Astronomical Features:** The temple's alignment with the Milky Way and the sun's position at the solstices.
