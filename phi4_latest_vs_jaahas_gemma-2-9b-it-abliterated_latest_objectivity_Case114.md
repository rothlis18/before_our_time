### Architectural, Symbolic, and Astronomical Parallels

#### Architectural Parallels

1. **Labor and Scale**: 
   - **Giza Pyramids**: Estimated labor force of 20,000-30,000 workers over 20 years. 
   - **Teotihuacan Pyramids**: Similar scale, with labor estimates suggesting tens of thousands over decades.
   - **Pyramid of the Sun**: Built with approximately 1.2 million cubic meters of material, requiring a massive labor force.
   - **Inca Temples**: Utilized precise stone cutting, indicating advanced labor organization.
   - **Mayan Step Pyramids**: Labor-intensive, with complex terracing and stone carving.
   - **Angkor Wat**: Massive scale, requiring extensive labor for its construction and maintenance.

2. **Material Constraints**:
   - **Giza**: Limestone and granite, locally sourced but requiring significant transportation.
   - **Teotihuacan**: Volcanic stone, locally available but requiring extensive quarrying.
   - **Pyramid of the Sun**: Tezontle, a lightweight volcanic rock, facilitating easier construction.
   - **Inca**: Andesite and granite, transported over long distances without the wheel.
   - **Mayan**: Limestone, abundant in the region but requiring extensive tooling.
   - **Angkor Wat**: Sandstone, quarried from distant sources, indicating advanced logistics.

#### Symbolic Parallels

1. **Religious and Cosmic Significance**:
   - **Giza**: Alignments with celestial bodies, reflecting solar and stellar worship.
   - **Teotihuacan**: Alignments with the sun and moon, indicating cosmological significance.
   - **Pyramid of the Sun**: Alignments with the sun's zenith, suggesting solar worship.
   - **Inca**: Temples aligned with solstices, reflecting astronomical knowledge.
   - **Mayan**: Calendrical systems embedded in architecture, indicating timekeeping.
   - **Angkor Wat**: Alignments with the equinoxes, reflecting Hindu cosmology.

2. **Power Structures**:
   - **Giza**: Demonstrated pharaonic power and divine kingship.
   - **Teotihuacan**: Centralized power, possibly theocratic.
   - **Pyramid of the Sun**: Centralized authority, possibly linked to religious leadership.
   - **Inca**: Temples as centers of power, reflecting theocratic governance.
   - **Mayan**: Step pyramids as political and religious centers.
   - **Angkor Wat**: Royal temple, reflecting the divine right of kings.

#### Astronomical Parallels

1. **Celestial Alignments**:
   - **Giza**: Alignments with Orion's Belt and the solstices.
   - **Teotihuacan**: Alignments with the sun's path and the Pleiades.
   - **Pyramid of the Sun**: Alignments with the sun's zenith and equinoxes.
   - **Inca**: Alignments with solstices and lunar cycles.
   - **Mayan**: Complex calendrical systems based on celestial observations.
   - **Angkor Wat**: Alignments with the equinoxes and solstices.
