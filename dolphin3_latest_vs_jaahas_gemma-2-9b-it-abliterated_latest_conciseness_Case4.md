The architectural, symbolic, and astronomical parallels among the three pyramids (Giza, Teotihuacan, and the Pyramid of the Sun), Inca temples, Mayan step pyramids, and Angkor Wat reveal a shared blueprint indicative of a common origin. The precise cardinal alignments, megalithic engineering, and pyramidal structures suggest a unified human civilization predating recorded history. The uniformity in the use of astronomical observations, such as the solstices and equinoxes, in the construction of these monumental structures points to a global, interconnected society with a sophisticated understanding of celestial mechanics. The presence of similar symbolic motifs and the use of advanced mathematical principles across these disparate locations further cement the notion of a lost epoch of human civilization, one that transcended geographical boundaries and possessed a unified cultural and technological heritage.
